One sealed snapshot of the protocol universe, — · source —. Every protocol that records no token and is not a wrapped, staked, bridged or versioned product of one that trades, ranked by the value a comparables model fitted on the snapshot's own traded protocols implies for it.

How the number is made

The model is fitted by the program, on this snapshot, every time it runs — nothing is carried over from another day or another dataset. It regresses the log market cap of the — traded protocols that record both a market cap and a TVL on four things a pre-token protocol also has: its TVL, its 7-day TVL growth, how long it has been listed, and how many chains it runs on. Each protocol category's family then adjusts the result by its own average miss, shrunk toward zero so a family with three members cannot shout. A row's implied value is its TVL × base multiple × growth factor × age factor, and every factor is printed on the row.

What the fit found

    Two guardrails keep one week or one birthday from deciding a launch price: the growth factor is bounded between ×0.50 and ×2.01, the age factor between ×0.55 and ×1.82. In the traded set, a week of explosive TVL growth carries a lower value per TVL — hot TVL reads as mercenary money — and long-listed protocols carry lower multiples too. The model prices both as the data has them, inside the bounds.

    Method notes. “Records no token” means the snapshot carries no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol for the protocol. The board applies the watchlist's own product rule to every row: categories Bridge, Canonical Bridge, Liquid Staking, Liquid Restaking, Restaked BTC and CEX are out, as are names ending in a version marker, names containing the whole word wrapped, staked or staking, and names beginning with an exchange's name — which is why WBTC and USDT0, though they record no token of their own, are not on this board: they are other tokens in another form. Ages are counted from the listing date to the snapshot date; a protocol with no listing date is scored at the traded set's median age. Growth is clamped to −60%…+120% before it enters the fit. Implied values are a model's estimate of a launch-day valuation, not a prediction that a token will exist.
